# Bao Do

M.S. candidate in Mechatronics and Robotics at NYU Tandon School of Engineering  
Expected graduation: May 2027

I build learning-enabled robotic systems that remain useful beyond idealized
simulation by accounting for state-estimation error, contact uncertainty,
actuator limits, and hardware constraints.

## Research directions

- Humanoid and legged locomotion
- Reinforcement learning and sim-to-real validation
- State estimation: IMU integration, EKF/ESKF, leg odometry, contact constraints
- Learning-based control under delayed, noisy, and partial observations
- Rehabilitation and assistive robotics: wearable sensing and adaptive feedback
